Common questions

Does my audio leave my device?

No. Recording and transcription both happen on your iPhone or iPad using Apple's on-device speech recognition.

Why can't I create an archive for someone?

TribeKeeper requires a recorded basis first — the person's consent, estate authorization, or an organizational policy. Set that on the People screen, then the archive becomes usable.

Why did the app decline to answer a question?

By design. When the archive contains nothing relevant, TribeKeeper says so instead of inventing an answer. Answers are grounded in real statements, with citations.

Why do some results appear for one person and not another?

Access level is applied to every search. Material marked internal or restricted is withheld from anyone whose access level does not include it.

How do I export or delete an archive?

Export is available per person from the People screen, in an open format. Deleting a person, a source, or an entire archive removes its transcripts and voice signatures. You can also email us to have data removed.

Is TribeKeeper the person?

No. It is software drawing on recordings, and it says so in every response. It should not be treated as the person's advice, instruction, or authorization.
