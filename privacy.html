Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 6, 2026

TribeBuilder ("we", "our", or "the app") is a social media management tool for music artists. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

TribeBuilder collects the following categories of information:

  • Account credentials: API keys you provide for AI services (stored locally on your device in the iOS Keychain, never transmitted to our servers).
  • Social media tokens: OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens for platforms you connect (Instagram, TikTok, X/Twitter, YouTube, Facebook). These are stored locally in the iOS Keychain.
  • Profile information: Display names, usernames, follower counts, and profile pictures retrieved from connected social media platforms.
  • Content you create: Text captions, AI-generated images, and post metadata created within the app. This content is stored locally on your device using SwiftData.
  • Usage data: We do not collect analytics, crash reports, or usage telemetry.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate with social media platforms on your behalf.
  • To publish content you create to your connected social media accounts.
  • To display your social media profile information and analytics within the app.
  • To generate AI-powered content using the AI service provider you select and configure.

3. Third-Party Services

TribeBuilder interacts with the following third-party services when you choose to use them:

  • Social media platforms: Instagram (Meta), TikTok, X (Twitter), YouTube (Google), Facebook (Meta), Spotify, Reddit. Data shared is limited to what is necessary for authentication and content publishing.
  • AI providers: Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI (ChatGPT/DALL-E), Google (Gemini), xAI (Grok). Prompts you generate are sent to the provider you select. We do not control how these providers process data — refer to their respective privacy policies.
  • Image hosting: Generated images are temporarily uploaded to Supabase Storage to provide public URLs required by social media APIs. Images are associated with anonymous UUIDs, not your personal information.

4. Data Storage and Security

  • All sensitive credentials (API keys, OAuth tokens) are stored in the iOS Keychain, which provides hardware-backed encryption.
  • Content data is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework.
  • We do not operate backend servers that store your personal data. The app communicates directly with third-party APIs.
  • Temporarily hosted images on Supabase Storage are stored in a public bucket and may be accessed by anyone with the URL. We recommend treating published image URLs as public content.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Data is only shared with the third-party services listed above, and only when you initiate an action (e.g., publishing a post, connecting a platform).

6. Your Rights

  • Disconnect platforms: You can disconnect any social media platform at any time from the app's Settings or Platforms tab. This removes stored tokens from your device.
  • Delete API keys: You can remove any AI service API key from the app's Settings tab.
  • Delete app data: Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data, including Keychain entries and SwiftData content.
  • Revoke access: You can revoke TribeBuilder's access from each social media platform's settings page independently.

7. Children's Privacy

TribeBuilder is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
