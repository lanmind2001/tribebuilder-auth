Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

TribeKeeper is operated by Brown Technologies Inc. The app preserves recorded conversations as a searchable, citable archive, so families and organizations can ask questions and receive answers grounded in what a person actually said.

Because that archive contains people's words and voices, privacy is a design constraint rather than a policy afterthought. This page explains what is stored, where it is processed, and the control you have over it.

Beta note. During TestFlight beta testing, the app operates on a built-in fictional demo archive. Recordings you make and their transcripts remain on your device, and no account is created. The sections below describe the app as a whole, including the optional server-backed archive.

Recording and transcription happen on your device

Audio is captured only while you are on the Record screen and have tapped Record. The app does not record in the background or when the app is closed. Speech is transcribed on your iPhone or iPad using Apple's on-device speech recognition; the audio is not sent to Apple or to us for transcription.

What the archive stores

When you choose to save material to an archive, TribeKeeper stores:

  • Transcribed statements and the text of imported material (for example emails or messages you supply), each attributed to a person.
  • Source information — title, date, type of source, and timestamps — so every answer can cite where it came from.
  • Identifiers used to attribute statements to the right person: display names, and where you supply them, email addresses and phone numbers.
  • Voice signatures, if you use voice matching (see below).
  • An activity log of consent changes, exclusions, identity merges, and archive queries, which you can review in the app.

Voice signatures (biometric information)

To identify a specific person's voice across many recordings, TribeKeeper can create a mathematical voice signature — a numeric representation of vocal characteristics. This is biometric information and is treated accordingly:

  • It is created only for people you deliberately enroll, and only to attribute statements to the correct person within your own archive.
  • Signatures for speakers who are not identified are discarded once processing finishes and are not retained.
  • Voice signatures are never sold, licensed, or disclosed to third parties, and are not used to identify anyone outside your archive.
  • They are deleted when you delete the person or the archive, and permanently within 30 days of your account being closed.
  • Voice matching is optional. Archives can be built without it.

Information about other people

An archive is, by nature, about someone other than you. TribeKeeper requires that a basis be recorded before an archive of a person can be created — either that person's consent while living, authorization from their estate or legal representative, or a documented organizational policy for workplace recordings. The app enforces this before an archive becomes usable.

You are responsible for having the right to the recordings and messages you supply, and for complying with the recording and consent laws that apply where you and the other participants are located. Some jurisdictions require every participant's consent to record a conversation.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data or the contents of your archive. We do not use advertising trackers, and the app contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs. We do not train artificial intelligence models on your archive. We do not track you across other apps or websites.

Service providers

If you connect the optional server-backed archive, its contents are stored using our infrastructure providers (Cloudflare) solely to operate the service. When you use the optional conversational feature with a language model, the relevant excerpts from your archive and your question are sent to that provider (Anthropic) to compose a response; they are not used to train models. We share information only as needed to operate the service, or when required by law.

How answers are generated

The conversational feature retrieves statements from your archive and, where enabled, uses a language model to phrase a response. Answers identify themselves as generated by software drawing on recordings, and distinguish material quoted from the archive — with citations — from inference in a person's style. When the archive does not cover a question, the app declines to answer rather than inventing one. TribeKeeper is not the person, does not speak for them, and its output should not be treated as their advice, instruction, or authorization.

Your control

  • Export. You can export an archive — statements, sources, and structure — in an open format at any time.
  • Exclude. Any source can be removed from the archive so it no longer appears in searches or answers.
  • Retire. An archive can be closed so it is preserved but no longer answers questions.
  • Delete. You can delete a person, a source, or an entire archive, including voice signatures and transcripts. Deletion requests may also be sent to the address below.

Retention

Archive content is kept until you delete it — the purpose of the product is preservation. Activity logs are retained for up to 24 months. When an account is closed, archive content and voice signatures are permanently deleted within 30 days.

Children

TribeKeeper is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. Archives of a deceased person are not intended for use by young children without a parent or guardian present.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the updated date above will change and, where the change affects how your archive is handled, we will notify you in the app.
