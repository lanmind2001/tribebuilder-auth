Cairnly — Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 12, 2026 · Provider: Hill Family Foundation

The short version. Cairnly is designed to be private. Your check‑ins, notes, and settings stay on your device. We have no server that receives your information, we use no advertising or analytics trackers, and we never sell or share your data.

What Cairnly stores, and where

Your mood check‑ins and any optional notes, your streak and history, and your preferences (such as your breathing pattern, reminder time, name, and whether scripture is shown) are saved locally on your device using the operating system's standard on‑device storage. This information is not transmitted to us or to any third party. If you delete the app, this information is removed with it.

Information we do NOT collect

  • We do not require an account, email, or login.
  • We do not collect your name, contacts, location, or device identifiers.
  • We do not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or third‑party trackers.
  • We do not sell or share your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, Cairnly schedules a local notification on your device at the time you choose. This is handled by your device's operating system; the reminder contains none of your personal information. You can turn reminders off in Settings or in your device settings at any time.

Crisis resources and outside links

Cairnly includes a "Help" feature and a resources list that can connect you to services such as the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, national helplines, and partner organizations, using your phone's standard calling, messaging, and web browser. When you choose to call, text, or open a link, that connection is made by your phone and the third party — not by Cairnly — and is subject to their terms and policies. Cairnly does not monitor, record, or transmit those communications.

Health‑related information

Some of what you record in Cairnly (such as moods or reflections) may be considered "consumer health data" under certain laws (for example, Washington's My Health My Data Act). Because this information stays on your device and is never collected by us, we do not use, sell, or share it. If this ever changes, we will update this policy, obtain any required consent, and provide the rights those laws require.

Children

Cairnly is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Security

Because your information stays on your device, its security is tied to your device's own protections (such as your passcode and device encryption). We recommend keeping your device locked and updated. No method of storage is perfectly secure, but Cairnly minimizes risk by not collecting or transmitting your data.

Your choices and rights

  • You can edit or remove your data at any time within the app, or by deleting the app.
  • You can turn reminders and scripture display on or off in Settings.
  • Because we do not collect or hold your data, in most cases there is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete on our side.

Changes to this policy

If we update Cairnly in a way that affects privacy, we will revise this policy and update the effective date, and where required we will ask for your consent.
