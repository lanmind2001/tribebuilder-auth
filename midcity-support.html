Getting in

MidCity Connect is a private community app. Access requires an invite code from the Mid City District. If you need one, just email us.

Forgot your password?

Message us and we will reset it for you. You will get a temporary password, then set your own under Account > Change Password.

Redeeming a deal

Open a deal in the app and show the cashier the QR code or the short code on screen.

Managing your account

Change your password or permanently delete your account any time under the Account tab.
