Common questions

Getting started

After installing, sign in and open Settings to set your name and connect the social platforms you post to. From the Create tab you can draft, schedule, and publish content. To explore the app first, tap Try TribeBuilder on the sign-in screen for a guided demo.

Connecting a platform

Go to More → Set Up Platforms (or AI Platforms for AI providers) and follow the step-by-step screens to connect Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, YouTube, X, and others. Each step includes a link to the provider. If a connection fails, disconnect and reconnect and make sure you granted all requested permissions during sign-in.

Adding an AI key

AI features use your own provider key (bring-your-own-key). In More → AI Platforms, pick a provider and follow the steps — each screen links directly to where you create the key. Keys are stored only in your device's secure Keychain and never leave your phone.

Protecting people in photos and videos

In editions that include media protection, faces are covered by default when you add a photo or video. Reveal a face only after confirming consent (guardian consent is required for anyone under 18). All of this happens on your device.

Your account and data

TribeBuilder stores your data on your device. You can sign out or permanently delete your account and data from Settings. For full details on removing your data, see our Data Deletion instructions.

Deleting your account: open Settings, find Delete Account, and confirm. This permanently removes your data and signs you out.
