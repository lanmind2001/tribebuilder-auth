Common questions

Getting started

After installing TribeBuilder, sign in and open Settings to set your name and connect the social platforms you post to. From the Create tab you can draft, schedule, and publish content.

Connecting a platform

Go to More → AI Platforms and the platform setup screens to connect Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, YouTube, X, and others. If a connection fails, disconnect and reconnect, and make sure you granted all requested permissions during sign-in.

Publishing or scheduling a post

Compose in the Create tab, choose your platforms, and either post now or pick a date and time to schedule. Scheduled posts appear in the Calendar tab.

Your account and data

TribeBuilder stores your data on your device. You can sign out or permanently delete your account and data at any time from More → Settings, at the bottom of the screen (“Delete Account”). For full details on removing your data, see our Data Deletion instructions.

Deleting your account: In the app, open More → Settings, scroll to the bottom, and tap Delete Account → Delete Everything. This permanently removes your data and signs you out.
